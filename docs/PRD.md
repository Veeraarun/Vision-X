# Vision-X

## AI Co-Therapist for Adaptive Virtual Reality Mental Healthcare

**Version:** 1.0  
**Hackathon MVP**  
**Team:** APEX  

---

## Product Vision

Vision-X is an AI-assisted Virtual Reality therapy platform that empowers therapists to conduct adaptive and personalized mental health therapy sessions.

Instead of replacing therapists, Vision-X acts as an intelligent co-therapist. It assists clinicians by generating personalized therapy plans, dynamically adapting immersive VR environments based on patient responses, monitoring physiological signals such as heart rate, and producing detailed post-session reports.

The system combines Artificial Intelligence, Virtual Reality, wearable sensors, and therapist expertise to deliver safer, more engaging, and data-driven mental healthcare.

---

## Problem Statement

Traditional mental health therapy often faces challenges including limited personalization, therapist workload, subjective progress tracking, and lack of immersive treatment methods.

Patients may struggle to visualize therapeutic scenarios or remain engaged during sessions.

Vision-X addresses these limitations by creating adaptive VR experiences under therapist supervision while continuously analyzing patient responses.
